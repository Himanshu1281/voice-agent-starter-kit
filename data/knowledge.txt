# Zryth Knowledge Base

## About Zryth
Zryth is an AI-first software development agency. We build industry-specific SaaS products and custom AI solutions to automate business workflows.

## Oswal AI
Oswal AI is our flagship educational SaaS product designed for schools and universities. 
Features include:
- Automated grading for multiple-choice and short-answer questions.
- Student performance tracking and analytics dashboards.
- AI-generated quiz questions based on uploaded syllabus PDFs.
Pricing:
- Basic Plan: ₹50,000 per year for up to 500 students.
- Pro Plan: ₹100,000 per year for unlimited students and priority support.

## Mill Management Software
Our Mill Management Software is tailored for textile and paper mills.
Features include:
- Real-time inventory tracking for raw materials.
- Shift scheduling and worker attendance tracking.
- Yield optimization analytics to reduce waste.
Pricing:
- Custom pricing starting at ₹25,000 per month based on mill size.

## Finance Auditor Software
Our Finance Auditor Software helps chartered accountants automate the auditing process.
Features include:
- Automated bank statement reconciliation.
- Anomaly detection for unusual expenses.
- One-click report generation for tax filings.
Pricing:
- Flat rate of ₹10,000 per month per CA firm.
